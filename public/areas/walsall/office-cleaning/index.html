The checklist

What a office and commercial cleaning covers

The same published checklist everywhere we work, Walsall included. Contract cleaning for offices, clinics, retail units and communal areas, worked around your opening hours rather than through them. One named supervisor you can ring directly.

Workspace

  • Desks, surfaces and shared equipment
  • Bins emptied and liners replaced
  • Floors vacuumed or mopped throughout
  • Glass partitions and internal doors
  • Meeting rooms reset

Washrooms

  • WCs, urinals and basins sanitised
  • Mirrors and chrome polished
  • Soap, towels and tissue restocked
  • Floors washed and disinfected
  • Sanitary waste contract arranged

Kitchen & break areas

  • Worktops, sinks and splashbacks
  • Fridge and microwave inside
  • Appliance exteriors
  • Crockery cleared and washed
  • Supplies checked and reordered

Communal

  • Entrances, lobbies and reception
  • Stairwells and corridors
  • Lift interiors and call panels
  • High-touch points sanitised
  • Doormats lifted and cleaned under

Specialist work — window cleaning above ground floor, deep kitchen extract cleaning, and waste contracts — is arranged separately. We can organise it alongside the contract.

Questions

Office and commercial cleaning, answered

Can you work outside our opening hours?
Yes — early mornings, evenings and weekends are normal for us, and out-of-hours cover is available for commercial contracts.
Are your staff checked?
Every one is DBS-checked and identity-verified before a first shift. We can supply the certificates for your records.
Do you supply consumables?
We can. Soap, paper, bin liners and washroom supplies can be included in the contract and restocked as part of the round.
What is the minimum contract?
There is no minimum term. We ask for a month's notice either way, which is enough time to hand over properly.
