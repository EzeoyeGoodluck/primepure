The checklist

What a end of tenancy cleaning covers

The same published checklist everywhere we work, Birmingham included. Cleaned to the inventory checklist your agent actually uses, photographed on completion, and re-done free within 72 hours if anything is flagged on the checkout report.

Kitchen

  • Oven, racks, trays and glass
  • Hob, extractor and filter
  • Fridge and freezer, inside
  • Cupboards inside and out
  • Sink, taps and descaling

Bathrooms

  • Limescale off tiles and screens
  • Grout and sealant treated
  • WC, cistern and behind
  • Extractor vents cleared
  • Mirrors and chrome polished

Bedrooms & living

  • Wardrobes and drawers inside
  • Behind and under furniture
  • Carpets vacuumed edge to edge
  • Marks lifted from walls
  • Windows inside, sills and tracks

Throughout

  • Skirtings, frames and doors
  • Light switches and sockets
  • Radiators, front and behind
  • Cobwebs and high corners
  • Bins washed, rubbish removed

Not included as standard: exterior windows, carpet extraction and upholstery. Each can be added — just say so when you enquire.

Questions

End of tenancy cleaning, answered

Will this get my deposit back?
Cleaning is one of the commonest deductions, and we clean to the checkout standard. We cannot speak for damage or wear, but if the report flags our cleaning we return within 72 hours free.
Does the property need to be empty?
Ideally yes — furniture and boxes get in the way of exactly the corners a checkout inspects. If it cannot be, tell us and we will plan round it.
Do you do the carpets too?
Vacuuming is included. Hot-water extraction, which some agreements require in writing, can be added to the booking.
Can the agent book directly?
Yes, and many do. Agents get one monthly invoice itemised by property rather than a bill per job.
