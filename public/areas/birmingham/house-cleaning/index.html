The checklist

What a house cleaning covers

The same published checklist everywhere we work, Birmingham included. Regular housekeeping or a one-off deep clean, carried out by the same two people each visit so nobody has to be shown round twice. Keys held securely if you would rather not be in.

Kitchen

  • Worktops, splashbacks and tiling
  • Hob, extractor and visible appliance fronts
  • Sink descaled, taps polished
  • Floors swept and washed
  • Bins emptied and wiped

Bathrooms

  • Bath, shower and screen descaled
  • WC cleaned inside and out
  • Basin, taps and mirrors polished
  • Tiles and grout wiped down
  • Floors washed

Living & bedrooms

  • Dusting throughout, including shelves and frames
  • Vacuuming carpets and rugs
  • Hard floors swept and mopped
  • Beds made or changed on request
  • Mirrors and glass tops

Throughout

  • Skirting boards and door frames
  • Light switches and handles
  • Cobwebs and high corners
  • Internal glass in doors
  • Tidying as we go

Not included as standard: inside the oven, inside windows above ground floor, carpet extraction and upholstery. All can be added — just ask when you enquire.

Questions

House cleaning, answered

Do I need to be in?
No. Most of our regular customers are at work. We can hold a key, use a key safe, or work around whoever is home.
Do you bring everything?
Yes, including the vacuum and the machines. If you prefer us to use your own products because of an allergy or a surface, just leave them out.
What if my regular cleaner is off?
You get a cover team who have been briefed from the notes we took on the first visit, and we tell you beforehand rather than surprising you.
Can I change or skip a week?
Yes. Twenty-four hours' notice and there is nothing to pay.
