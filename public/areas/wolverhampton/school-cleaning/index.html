The checklist

What a school and nursery cleaning covers

The same published checklist everywhere we work, Wolverhampton included. Daily cleaning for primary and secondary schools, nurseries and colleges, worked around the timetable rather than through it. Every member of staff on a school site holds an Enhanced DBS, and the same team comes back so you are not re-inducting strangers every week.

Classrooms

  • Desks, chairs and table tops sanitised
  • High-touch points: handles, switches, rails
  • Whiteboards, trays and shelving
  • Floors swept, mopped or vacuumed
  • Bins emptied and liners replaced

Washrooms & changing

  • WCs, urinals and basins disinfected
  • Low-level pupil fittings not missed
  • Soap, paper and tissue restocked
  • Floors washed with virucidal detergent
  • Sanitary and nappy waste handled

Halls, corridors & offices

  • Main halls and dining spaces reset
  • Corridors, stairwells and entrances
  • Reception and admin offices
  • Glass doors and internal partitions
  • Matting lifted and cleaned under

Kitchens & food areas

  • Servery and dining tables after service
  • Staff room worktops and appliances
  • Fridges and microwaves inside
  • Floors degreased
  • Food-area cloths kept separate

Specialist work is arranged separately and quoted on its own: window cleaning above ground floor, kitchen extract canopy cleaning, playground jet washing and clinical waste contracts.

How it runs
  1. 01A site walk and a written specWe walk the building with your site manager and agree exactly what is cleaned, how often and at what time. That written schedule is what the team works to, and what we are inspected against.
  2. 02Around the timetableMost of the work happens after the last bell or before the first. Nurseries and wraparound care often need a mid-afternoon pass as well, which we build into the round.
  3. 03Enhanced DBS and safeguardingEveryone on site holds an Enhanced DBS, signs in through your visitor system and follows your safeguarding policy. Certificates go to your office before a first shift.
  4. 04Holidays are for the deep workHalf terms and the summer are when floors get stripped and sealed, carpets extracted and the jobs done that cannot happen in term time.
Questions

School and nursery cleaning, answered

Are your staff Enhanced DBS-checked?
Everyone who sets foot on a school or nursery site, without exception. We send the certificates to your office before a first shift and keep the renewals current.
Can you work around the school day?
Yes, and we expect to. Most school rounds run after the last bell or before the first. Nurseries and wraparound care usually need a mid-afternoon pass too.
How do you stop cross-contamination?
Colour-coded cloths and mops, one colour per area, never crossed between washrooms, classrooms and food areas. Staff are trained on it and supervisors check it.
Do you do holiday deep cleans?
That is when most of the heavy work happens — floor stripping and sealing, carpet extraction, high-level dusting and anything else that cannot be done with pupils in the building.
What about an outbreak?
We can put on an additional virucidal fogging or touch-point pass at short notice. Tell us in the morning and we will usually cover it that evening.
