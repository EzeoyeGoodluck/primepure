The checklist

What a carpet and floor cleaning covers

The same published checklist everywhere we work, West Bromwich included. Hot-water extraction with industrial machines, not a domestic shampooer. It lifts the dirt that has worked down into the pile rather than just cleaning the top of it.

Before

  • Pile inspected and fibre identified
  • Furniture moved where safe to do so
  • Thorough dry vacuum first
  • Stains pre-treated and left to dwell
  • Edges and corners hand-worked

Extraction

  • Hot-water extraction through the pile
  • Traffic lanes given a second pass
  • Odour neutraliser where needed
  • Pile groomed in one direction
  • Moisture lifted out, not left in

Also cleaned

  • Stairs and landings
  • Sofas and armchairs
  • Dining chairs and mattresses
  • Rugs, on site or taken away
  • Hard floors and grout lines

After

  • Furniture protected until dry
  • Airflow set up to speed drying
  • Aftercare explained before we go
  • Any remaining marks pointed out honestly

Not included as standard: lifting and refitting fitted carpets, repairs, or treating subfloor damp. We will tell you if we find any of it.

Questions

Carpet and floor cleaning, answered

Will every stain come out?
No, and anyone who promises otherwise is guessing. Dye, bleach and old pet damage can be permanent. We will tell you what we expect to shift before we start.
How long until I can walk on it?
A few hours for light use in a warm, ventilated room. Leave heavy furniture off it until it is fully dry.
Is it safe with pets and children?
Yes. The products are non-toxic and the extraction removes the solution rather than leaving residue behind.
Do you move furniture?
We move what two people can move safely. Anything very heavy, fragile or electrical we clean around.
