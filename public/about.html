About us

A Walsall cleaning company, working the way we would want it done.

PrimePure runs from No. 2 Dudley Street in Walsall, covering nine towns across the West Midlands and south Staffordshire. Domestic rounds, tenancy handovers, carpet work and office contracts.

What we are trying to do differently

Most cleaning companies tell you they are reliable and thorough. Everyone says that, so it is worth nothing. Here is what we do instead that you can actually check.

We publish the checklist. Every service page lists what is covered and what is not, so there is no argument afterwards about what was included.

We give one number for the guarantee. Seventy-two hours, everywhere on this site. Not forty-eight on one page and seventy-two on another.

We send the same team. For regular work you get the same two people, who learn your house and stop needing to be told.

We say no when we should. If we cannot get a team to you at a sensible time, or a stain is not going to come out, we say so before you book.
