Everything that gets cleaned

Washrooms

How it runs

What actually happens

We book the job, not a block of hours, so nobody is watching a clock while the oven soaks. Here is the order it goes in.

  1. 01A site visit and a written specWe walk the building with you and write down exactly what is covered, how often, and at what time. It becomes the schedule the team works to.
  2. 02The same staff, vetted and badgedEvery operative is DBS-checked and trained to COSHH standards. You get the same faces, so they learn the building.
  3. 03A supervisor, not a call centreOne named contact with a direct number, plus a monthly check against the spec so standards do not drift.

Questions

What people ask us

Can you work outside our opening hours?
Yes — early mornings, evenings and weekends are normal for us, and out-of-hours cover is available for commercial contracts.
Are your staff checked?
Every one is DBS-checked and identity-verified before a first shift. We can supply the certificates for your records.
Do you supply consumables?
We can. Soap, paper, bin liners and washroom supplies can be included in the contract and restocked as part of the round.
What is the minimum contract?
There is no minimum term. We ask for a month's notice either way, which is enough time to hand over properly.
